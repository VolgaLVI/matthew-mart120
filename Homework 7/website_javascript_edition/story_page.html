Short Story:

Alligators aren't known for being able to communicate. So we think.
After much observation, researchers have determined that few members of the species can, in fact, communicate with one another.
What else could explain the phenomenon where one specifical alligator makes a small noise and all other alligators stop and look at it as it makes noises for the next dozen of minutes?
The researchers decided to name this Alligator "Story". After all, he seemed to be telling stories to the other alligators.
You see, Alligators are rather long animals. They aren't known for their height.
If you were trying to insult him, I suppose you could call him a short story
